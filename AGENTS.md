Reliability repairs must preserve established sources, SAGE analysis, scoring, and customer flows. Do not replace a capability with a weaker implementation merely to make a refresh or test pass.

Changes that replace a source, remove a capability, or redesign the customer experience require a concrete proposal and explicit user authorization before implementation. Honor authorization already given in the conversation.

Newswire must retain verified reporter/X posts and official NFL/team reporting, relevant player information, source provenance, publication dates when verified, and specific fantasy implications. Generic resource links and repeated boilerplate do not satisfy this contract. Automated source discovery supplements editorial reports; it must not overwrite them.

Report deployment, refresh readiness, and verified customer behavior separately. Test the product contract and verify production outcomes before claiming completion.
