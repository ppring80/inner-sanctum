# Columbia Evidence Reconciliation V1

As tributaries multiply, Super SAGE must not double-count correlated evidence.

Example:
poor pass EPA + high pass yards allowed + favorable WR matchup may all partly describe the same defensive weakness. They can corroborate one another, but they are not three independent reasons to triple-weight the matchup.

Before reasoning, reconciliation groups evidence by underlying concept and identifies:
- corroboration
- contradiction
- staleness
- overlapping signals

Contradictions route to SAGE Skeptic. Fresher verified evidence outranks stale evidence. Missing evidence remains uncertainty.

This layer increases analytical discipline without creating a new ranking score.
