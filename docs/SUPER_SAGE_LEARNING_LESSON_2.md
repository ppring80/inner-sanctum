## Learned principles

LB2-H1 — HOLDOUT PASS:
For WR and TE, recent target opportunity is a stronger next-week ranking signal than recent touchdown production and modestly stronger than recent fantasy points.

LB2-H2 — HOLDOUT PASS:
Recent touchdowns are a materially noisier forward signal than recent opportunity for WR/TE. SAGE should not chase TD-driven box-score spikes without supporting workload.

LB2-H3 — HOLDOUT PASS WITH POSITION-SPECIFIC INTERPRETATION:
RB rushing workload and rushing production are highly persistent next-week signals. Receiving opportunity adds information but is not a universal replacement for carry workload in Half-PPR.

LB2-H4 — INCONCLUSIVE / NEEDS RICHER QB FEATURES:
Passing yards/TDs alone do not outperform recent total QB fantasy production. Add QB rushing opportunity, sacks/pressure, designed runs and opponent context before drawing a stronger QB conclusion.
