# Super SAGE Customer Response Contract — 1/3/10 + Opt-In Depth

Applies to every Super SAGE recommendation surface: QB, RB, WR, TE, FLEX, lineup/start-sit, waiver and FAAB. Additional positions/tools should inherit the same contract.

## Default response

1 second: verdict/direction.
3 seconds: primary plain-language reason.
10 seconds: concise evidence, fantasy impact, uncertainty/watch item and confidence.

Then ask exactly one progressive-disclosure question:
**Would you like the deeper SAGE analysis on [subject]?**

Customer choices:
- Yes — show me the details
- No — keep it simple

If No: stop. Do not continue explaining.
If Yes: continue with relevant advanced evidence without repeating the summary.

## Advanced detail is position-aware

QB: coverage splits, man/zone, MOFO/MOFC, blitz, pressure, four-man pressure, EPA/dropback, success, CPOE, scramble/sack response, personnel/motion/play-action interaction.

RB: box/front tendencies, carry/target/snap/route role, run-concept interaction, red-zone/goal-line work, receiving matchup, game-script sensitivity.

WR: man/zone and coverage-family splits, outside/slot alignment, target share, air yards, route/opportunity quality, pressure-driven redistribution, opponent role coverage.

TE: middle-open/closed, LB/safety matchup, personnel, seam/intermediate opportunity, target/route/red-zone usage.

FLEX/start-sit: expose the evidence that materially separates the candidates rather than dumping every available metric.

Waiver/FAAB: role change evidence, opportunity quality, injury/depth-chart catalyst, schedule/matchup context, market/budget logic and uncertainty.

## Evidence rules

Advanced detail must preserve sample size/timeframe/source when available. Never manufacture an advanced metric because the customer asked for more. Hypotheses remain labeled and cannot masquerade as current facts.

The sophisticated analysis stays behind SAGE; the customer controls depth.
