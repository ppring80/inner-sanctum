# The Columbia — Super SAGE Weekly Evidence Packet V1

The Columbia is the convergence layer. Tributaries remain independently sourced; the Columbia packages them for reasoning.

Per-player tributaries:
- Weekly SAGE ranking/recommendation (ranking authority)
- current evidence
- position-specific matchup intelligence
- historical context

Readiness flags make missing water visible instead of silently filling gaps.

The Columbia does not alter production rankings. It gives Super SAGE one consistent, auditable packet from which to produce the 1/3/10 recommendation and optional deeper analysis.

Initial supported positions: QB/RB/WR/TE.
