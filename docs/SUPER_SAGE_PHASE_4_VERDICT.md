# Super SAGE Phase 4 — Verdict V1

Verdict V1 translates a cleared Super SAGE analysis packet into the Inner Sanctum 1/3/10-second model.

## 1 second
Direction/status.

## 3 seconds
Primary verified reason.

## 10 seconds
- current evidence + source/time/sample
- football reasoning
- research hypotheses explicitly labeled
- uncertainty
- what could change the verdict

## Critical boundary
Verdict V1 does not invent a player winner from football theory. A start/sit, waiver, FAAB or player-ranking decision still requires the appropriate authoritative SAGE/player evidence. This layer explains and contextualizes; it does not recalculate Weekly SAGE.
