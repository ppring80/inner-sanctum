# Super SAGE Historical Lab V2

Adds raw play-level context for 2016 through 2026 Week 3 while preserving V1.

V2 keeps high-value football/fantasy fields: game state, down/distance, field position, play type, dropback/rush/scramble/sack, air yards, YAC, player IDs, shotgun/no-huddle/play action, red-zone proxies, conversions, EPA/WPA/success and available completion/XYAC fields.

Raw source remains nflverse; generated corpus is an Actions artifact rather than committed bulk data.

V2 does not change production rankings. It enables richer historical experiments and role/context features.
