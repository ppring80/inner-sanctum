# Super SAGE — Football Knowledge Program

## Goal

SAGE should develop broad football knowledge: history, rules, strategy, schemes, coaching philosophies, coaching trees, positions, terminology, situational football, player development and the evolution of the game. That knowledge should improve fantasy analysis only where evidence supports the connection.

## Curriculum

1. Origins and history of American football; rule evolution; major eras and innovations.
2. Offensive foundations: personnel, formations, run game, passing game, protections, route concepts, motion/shifts, tempo and situational offense.
3. Defensive foundations: fronts, gaps, fits, leverage, man/zone, coverage families, match coverage, pressure, disguise and situational defense.
4. Special teams and field-position strategy.
5. Coaching philosophies and coaching trees, including historical systems and modern descendants.
6. Position-specific responsibilities and player archetypes.
7. Modern analytics and tracking concepts.
8. Scheme interaction and opponent-quality context.
9. Translation to fantasy opportunity and outcomes.
10. Historical archive: teams, coaches, players, landmark games/seasons, innovations and changes in strategy.

## Source policy

Prefer authoritative/primary sources where possible: official rulebooks, coaching materials legally available for use, reputable books supplied or licensed by the owner, interviews/clinics, league/team material and high-quality analytical datasets. Store concise derived knowledge and provenance, not copied books.

## Promotion policy

CONCEPT/HISTORICAL_KNOWLEDGE can inform explanation. HYPOTHESIS cannot change rankings merely because it is plausible. Fantasy-impact logic must pass documented validation before becoming VALIDATED_INSIGHT and influencing production SAGE.

## First module

Coverage + motion: safety shells, man/zone evidence, post-snap rotation and motion response. This module intentionally starts with football understanding before fantasy weighting.
