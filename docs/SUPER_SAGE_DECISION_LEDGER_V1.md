# Super SAGE Decision Ledger V1

The Decision Ledger is the live-learning memory for the remainder of 2026.

At decision time record:
- player/week/context
- recommendation and confidence
- trigger (weekly or material change)
- immutable Columbia evidence snapshot

After the game append:
- actual fantasy outcome
- outcome evidence
- diagnosis

Never rewrite history after seeing the result. A material-change reanalysis creates a new linked decision rather than overwriting the earlier call.

Review distinguishes recommendation-quality failures from explanation-quality failures.

The ledger generates learning cases/hypotheses; production ranking logic changes only after historical/live validation.
