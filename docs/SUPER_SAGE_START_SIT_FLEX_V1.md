# Super SAGE Start/Sit + FLEX Intelligence V1

Purpose: converge QB/RB/WR/TE matchup intelligence into customer decisions without creating a second ranking model.

Authority:
- Weekly SAGE owns ordering/preference.
- Super SAGE owns explanation/evidence depth.

Super SAGE compares only evidence that materially separates candidates: role stability, opportunity quality, scheme interaction, stress exposure, scoring paths, risk/confidence, sample and source.

Unlike positions are not forced onto a new synthetic score.

Customer contract:
1 second — START [player].
3 seconds — the reason that most separates the candidates.
10 seconds — why preferred, primary risk, confidence.
Then: Would you like the deeper SAGE comparison?

No = stop. Yes = reveal verified position-aware evidence.
