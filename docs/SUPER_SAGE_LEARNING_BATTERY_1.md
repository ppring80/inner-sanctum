# Super SAGE Learning Battery #1

Outcome: next-week Half-PPR. All predictor features are shifted so the current week's final outcome is never used to predict itself.

Chronological split:
2019-22 discovery; 2023 validation; 2024-25 untouched holdout; 2026 Weeks 1-3 prospective.

Initial findings:
- H1 HOLDOUT PASS: 3-game recent fantasy production is more stable/predictive than immediately prior-week fantasy output across QB/RB/WR/TE.
- H2 HOLDOUT PASS: RB carry volume and WR/TE target volume over the recent 3-game window show stronger next-week rank signal than immediately prior-week fantasy points.
- H3 REJECTED: QB passing-attempt volume alone does not outperform recent QB fantasy production.

Interpretation boundary:
These are predictive associations, not causal claims. They do not automatically change Weekly SAGE rankings. Next step is incremental-value testing against SAGE's existing projection/ranking features and stronger controls for games played, role changes, opponent, injury and team context.
