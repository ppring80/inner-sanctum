# Super SAGE WR Matchup Intelligence V1

Joins defensive coverage structure with receiver opportunity quality.

Core evidence:
targets/game, target share, air yards, air-yard share, aDOT, explosive-target rate, slot/outside alignment when sourced, and target redistribution under QB disruption when available.

Rules:
coverage tendency is not itself a WR advantage; targets/air yards are opportunity rather than guaranteed production; missing player-vs-coverage splits remain missing.

Customer presentation: 1/3/10 summary, then opt-in advanced coverage/alignment/opportunity detail.
