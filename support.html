Get Support

Inner Sanctum customer support is available through the Get in Touch contact form on the Inner Sanctum website.

Use the form for product support, connection problems, bug reports, feature questions, privacy requests, or questions about Inner Sanctum SAGE.

Go to the Inner Sanctum contact form

The contact form asks for your name, email address, the subject of your request, and a message so that Pringle AI LLC can respond to your inquiry.

Please do not send fantasy-provider passwords, authentication tokens, session cookies, or other sensitive credentials in a support request.

ChatGPT Integration Support

If your question concerns Inner Sanctum SAGE for ChatGPT, please mention that you were using the ChatGPT integration and identify the SAGE capability involved, such as player analysis, player comparison, weekly rankings, draft outlook, linked-league information, or lineup recommendations.

Connected-League Support

For connected-league issues, tell us which supported fantasy provider you were attempting to use and describe the connection or synchronization issue.

Do not include your fantasy-provider password or other private authentication credentials.

Privacy Requests

The Inner Sanctum contact form may also be used to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information maintained by Inner Sanctum, subject to applicable law and legitimate security or legal retention requirements.

Additional information about how Inner Sanctum handles information is available in the Inner Sanctum Privacy Policy .
