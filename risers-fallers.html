What Counts As A Riser?
📈 A riser gained meaningful target share from the previous week while maintaining enough playing time for the change to matter.
📉 A faller lost meaningful target share from the previous week. That can signal a shrinking role — but context matters.
Read The Whole Signal
🎯 Target share tells you how much of the passing offense flowed through the player.
🏈 Snap share helps distinguish a real role change from a one-week target spike or dip.
🔢 Targets give the raw volume behind the percentage movement.
A one-week fall for an established elite player is not automatically a sell signal. Players marked TOP 50 ADP deserve extra context because normal weekly variance can look dramatic.
