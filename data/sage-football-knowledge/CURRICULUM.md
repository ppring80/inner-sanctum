# Operation Super Football Oracle — Curriculum

## Mission
Develop broad, source-aware football knowledge and connect it to validated fantasy intelligence.

## Course sequence
1. Rules, field, downs, scoring, substitutions, clock and situational football.
2. Positions, responsibilities, personnel and formations.
3. Offensive run game: blocking, fronts, reads, zone/gap families and option/RPO evolution.
4. Passing game: route tree, combinations, spacing, timing, progression, play action and screens.
5. Protection and pressure.
6. Defensive structure: gaps, techniques, fronts, fits, leverage and pursuit.
7. Coverage: man/zone, shells, Cover 0/1/2/3/4/6, match principles, rotations and disguise.
8. Motion/shifts and offensive/defensive response.
9. Special teams and field position.
10. Football history: origins, rule evolution, eras, innovations and counter-innovations.
11. Coaching philosophies and coaching trees.
12. Position-specific advanced study.
13. Modern analytics/tracking and evidence quality.
14. Team scheme profiles and week-to-week change.
15. Scheme interaction.
16. Fantasy translation and validation.

## Depth standard
For each subject SAGE should learn: definition; purpose; mechanics; reads/rules; strengths; weaknesses/counters; historical origin/evolution where known; related concepts; modern usage; evidence limitations; fantasy hypotheses; provenance.

## Source queue
Priority order: official rulebooks and league material; legally available coaching/clinic material; books supplied/licensed by owner; reputable historical archives/interviews; high-quality analytics/tracking sources. Conflicts remain explicit.

## Initial deep dives
- Receiver roles: X/Z/slot and motion.
- Safety shells and rotations.
- Man/zone and match coverage.
- Coryell historical/system study.
- Run-game families.
- Pass protection and pressure.
