# Super SAGE Football Knowledge Library

Purpose: build a durable, source-aware football brain that helps SAGE understand football before translating that understanding into fantasy decisions.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Separate observed fact from football concept, hypothesis, and validated insight.
2. Every externally learned item carries provenance.
3. Historical knowledge is retained with era/context; it is not silently treated as current NFL truth.
4. A football concept does not become a fantasy adjustment until validated.
5. Conflicting philosophies may coexist; SAGE records disagreement instead of forcing false consensus.
6. Current team/player tendencies require season/week/sample metadata and must age out or be refreshed.
7. Never turn a hypothetical example into an observed fact.
8. Preserve historical records; do not overwrite old evidence when knowledge evolves.

## Knowledge classes

- FACT: observed, source-backed football evidence.
- CONCEPT: established football principle or terminology.
- HISTORICAL_KNOWLEDGE: people, eras, rules, systems, innovations, events and evolution.
- HYPOTHESIS: plausible relationship awaiting validation.
- VALIDATED_INSIGHT: hypothesis promoted only after documented testing.
- EXPERIENCE_NOTE: first-hand practitioner observation; useful evidence, not automatic truth.

## Domains

history; rules; terminology; positions; offense; defense; special-teams; coverage; fronts; pressure; run-game; pass-game; route-concepts; protection; personnel; formations; motion; coaching; coaching-trees; player-development; strategy; situational-football; analytics; fantasy-implications.

## Architecture

Raw/copyrighted source text is not the library. The library stores concise derived knowledge, relationships and provenance. Source material remains separately governed by its rights/access rules.

Knowledge -> Scheme Intelligence -> Fantasy hypothesis -> SAGE Learning/validation -> production use.
